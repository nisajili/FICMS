# FICMS — Patient Portal Guide

The Patient Portal lets you manage your fertility care in one place. Only
approved, released information is shown. Internal clinical notes, counseling
notes, donor identities, unreleased results, and HR records are **never** shown.

## Getting access

You will receive an invitation (or can be approved to self-register). Use the
link/credentials provided by your clinic to sign in. Set up two-factor
authentication for extra security if prompted.

## What you can do

- **Profile**: update permitted details and manage your partner link.
- **Appointments**: request and view appointments; see your schedule.
- **Forms**: complete assigned medical/consent forms.
- **Treatment timeline**: follow your cycle and treatment milestones.
- **Consent**: review and sign consent documents; once signed they are recorded.
- **Results**: view released laboratory and imaging results (never unreleased).
- **Billing**: view invoices, installment balances, and make supported online
  payments; download receipts.
- **Documents**: download approved records and letters.
- **Messages & notifications**: receive secure messages and control
  notification preferences (email/SMS/WhatsApp).

## Privacy & responsibilities

- Keep your password and 2FA codes private.
- Contact your clinic if you notice an error in your record — corrections are
  versioned and never silently overwrite a signed record.
- Your clinic controls data retention per applicable law.

## Getting help

For any account, access, or data question, contact your clinic's front desk or
administrator. For emergencies, contact your clinic directly — do not rely on
the portal.
